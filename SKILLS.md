# Chronomaster — Skill Overview

All skills of the **Chronomaster** (class **Timekeeper**) at a glance, so you can see them without installing the mod.
Numbers are from version **1.73** (test version). The balance isn't tuned yet, so values may change.

**"Per rank"** means the value multiplies with your points. Most skills have 5 ranks, capstones and special skills have 1.
The **anchor** is where your Time Bomb landed, which is also the spot you return to.

---

## Action Skill — Time Bomb

*What you did stays done, what happened to you gets undone.* (Inspired by the Time Bomb from Black Ops 2 Zombies.)

1. **Throw** the bomb. Where it lands becomes your **anchor**, and it saves your **health, shield and magazine** at that moment.
2. **Fight** for up to **20 seconds**.
3. **Press again** and you snap back to the anchor with the health, shield and magazine you had.
   Your **kills, damage and loot stay**.

- **Cooldown:** 35 seconds.
- **Expired:** If you don't return in time, the bomb expires. You stay where you are, and only **half the cooldown** applies.
- **Vehicles:** You can't throw or detonate while in a vehicle.

### Double shots

*Echo Bullet* and *Dimensional Loop* fire a real second shot right after yours. The extra shot costs no ammo. With burst-fire weapons, the whole burst is repeated. If both skills land on the same shot, you still get one extra shot, not two.

### Haste Stacks

- Every kill adds a stack. Every 2 seconds of sustained fire adds one too, up to 10 from firing.
- At most 20 stacks in total.
- Starting 3 seconds after your last new stack, one stack decays every second, the ones from firing first.
- The number of stacks shows on your HUD.

### Grenade damage

*Reverberation*, *Time Vortex* and *Parting Gift* use the damage number shown on your **equipped grenade's card**. Grenades without a damage number (e.g. *Sky Rocket*) make them weak.
